Proof of Work

A closer look at how I work

The examples below are illustrative sample projects, presented to show approach and process rather than confidential client work.

Sample project · Case Management

Organizing a backlog of active case files

The Challenge

An attorney's active caseload had grown faster than the filing system could keep up with, making it hard to see what needed attention next.

What I Did

Rebuilt the case-tracking structure, standardized file naming and status labels, and set up a simple weekly review to flag upcoming deadlines.

The Result

A clear, current view of every case's status, with nothing falling through the cracks between reviews.

Sample project · Calendar & Scheduling

Untangling a double-booked calendar

The Challenge

A busy principal's calendar had conflicting meetings and no buffer time, making preparation difficult.

What I Did

Audited and reorganized the calendar, built in preparation and travel buffers, and set up a intake process for new meeting requests.

The Result

A predictable weekly rhythm with room to prepare, and no more scheduling conflicts.

Sample project · Client Communication

Keeping clients informed during a busy period

The Challenge

Clients were left waiting for updates during a particularly high-volume stretch of work.

What I Did

Set up a consistent update cadence and templates for common status communications, while keeping each message personal to the client.

The Result

Clients reported feeling informed and reassured, and inbound status-check emails dropped noticeably.
